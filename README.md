### CHEM-000440: An isolable molecular antimony-mononitride adduct
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Stabilize an intact SbN unit in an isolable molecular adduct. Establish its bonding and isotope-sensitive vibrational signature.
- **Status:** Gas-phase SbN spectroscopy dates to Coy and Sponer’s 1940 experiments. Qian and colleagues’ 2026 primary comparison lists SbN as detected but not isolated.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Isolable samples would test how coordination stabilizes a heavy-element nitride.
- **Key works:** N. H. Coy and H. Sponer (1940), “Emission Spectrum of Antimony Nitride,” [DOI: 10.1103/PhysRev.58.709](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.58.709); W. Qian et al. - **Related:** Relativistic molecular physics—stability of heavy-element multiple bonds.
